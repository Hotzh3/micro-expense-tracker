# Demo Data Plan

Use this dataset to make Pocket Leak look consistent across screenshots, demos, and portfolio material.

## Expense Mix

### Coffee

- `Starbucks Reforma` - `85.50`
- `Café local` - `42.00`

### Food

- `Restaurante Toks` - `430.00`
- `Tacos al pastor` - `128.00`
- `Snack convenience` - `65.00`

### Transport

- `Uber trip` - `72.50`
- `Metro / taxi` - `35.00`

### Shopping

- `Amazon` - `199.00`
- `OXXO` - `140.00`

### Entertainment

- `Spotify` - `129.00`
- `Netflix` - `219.00`

## Goals

- Weekly goal: `1,200`
- Monthly goal: `4,800`

## Expected Insights

- Coffee and food should be the top categories.
- Shopping should appear as a smaller but visible share.
- Transport should show steady small purchases.
- Entertainment should show recurring subscriptions.
- Monthly projection should remain believable and not extreme.

## Expected Widgets

- Show weekly spend, remaining amount, and goal status.
- Show recent spend trend.
- Keep the widget clean with a couple of highlighted numbers only.

## Suggested Demo Story

1. A user buys coffee, lunch, and a ride.
2. Pocket Leak captures those purchases quickly.
3. Dashboard shows the running totals.
4. Insights reveal coffee and food as the main leaks.
5. Goals show the user close to a weekly limit.
6. Export generates a local PDF or CSV summary.
