# Design Direction

## Visual Identity

J Tap should feel like a calm, premium, Apple-like utility with a strict black-and-white palette.

## Core Aesthetic

- Black background
- White typography
- Transparent or glass-like panels
- Subtle gray borders
- Simple spacing and restrained motion
- Minimal visual noise

## Logo Concept

The logo direction is a white "J" on a black background. It should feel immediate, memorable, and app-icon friendly.

## UI Principles

- Keep the main action obvious
- Make the quick-add flow feel fast
- Use strong typography hierarchy
- Avoid decorative clutter
- Prefer reusable, consistent components

## Component Language

- Glass cards for grouping content
- Pills for categories
- Clear primary action button
- Compact metric cards for summary data
- Empty states that are calm and informative

## Portfolio Quality Bar

The design should look intentionally crafted rather than generic. The goal is to show product taste, restraint, and native iOS sensibility.
