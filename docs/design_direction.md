# Design Direction

## Visual Identity

Pocket Leak should feel calm, premium, and controlled. The visual system is intentionally minimal so the product reads as a serious utility rather than a flashy finance app.

## Core Aesthetic

- dark shell with white typography
- glass-like cards and subtle borders
- restrained accent colors only where they carry meaning
- compact hierarchy for totals, trends, and cards
- motion that helps state changes, not decoration

## Product Tone

- precise
- local-first
- privacy-aware
- confidence over noise

## Component Language

- summary cards for totals
- donut and trend charts for quick reading
- pills for categories and filters
- simple list rows for history
- explicit empty states that teach the next action

## Portfolio Requirements

The interface should look deliberate in screenshots and in a one-minute demo. That means:

- no crowded panels
- no generic purple defaults
- no unnecessary gradients
- no heavy visual clutter
- no design choices that hide real product structure

## Screenshot Rule

Every screenshot should make the app feel like a real shipped product, even though the current goal is portfolio presentation rather than App Store release.
