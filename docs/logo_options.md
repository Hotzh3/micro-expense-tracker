# Pocket Leak Logo Options

## 1. Minimal PL Monogram

- Visual idea: A tight `PL` monogram in white on black, built from straight lines and rounded corners.
- Why it fits: It feels restrained, premium, and easy to recognize at small app-icon sizes.
- Pros:
  - Very legible as an app icon
  - Matches the app's minimalist identity
  - Works well in light and dark contexts
- Cons:
  - Less literal than a leak or pocket symbol
  - Can feel generic if the letterform is not distinctive enough

## 2. White Droplet / Leak Icon

- Visual idea: A simple white droplet or drip shape on a black field, possibly with a subtle cutout.
- Why it fits: It directly communicates the "leak" part of Pocket Leak.
- Pros:
  - Instantly readable
  - Strong visual metaphor
  - Easy to scale into iconography
- Cons:
  - Can feel too literal or commodity-like
  - May not clearly reference money without extra context

## 3. Pocket Outline With Small Drip

- Visual idea: A pocket silhouette with a tiny drip or streak escaping from the opening.
- Why it fits: It connects the product name to the spending problem in a single mark.
- Pros:
  - More original than a plain droplet
  - Balances metaphor and meaning
  - Distinctive if drawn with strong geometry
- Cons:
  - More complex than a monogram
  - Can become visually busy at small sizes

## 4. Coin With Leak Drop

- Visual idea: A small coin circle with a controlled drip escaping from one edge.
- Why it fits: It makes the financial meaning explicit while keeping the icon minimal.
- Pros:
  - Strong money association
  - Clear story in one icon
  - Good option for marketing illustrations
- Cons:
  - Risk of looking too illustrative
  - Less timeless than a simple monogram

## 5. Lowercase `p` As Pocket / Leak

- Visual idea: A custom lowercase `p` that doubles as a pocket shape, with the bowl or stem suggesting a drip.
- Why it fits: It can create a memorable brand mark while staying very minimal.
- Pros:
  - Brand-forward and modern
  - Subtle enough for a premium utility app
  - Good candidate for a future wordmark
- Cons:
  - Harder to execute cleanly
  - May not read instantly without the product name nearby

## Recommended App Icon Direction

Recommended direction: start with the Minimal PL Monogram as the app icon and keep the Droplet / Leak Icon as the strongest backup if a more literal symbol is wanted later.

If a temporary placeholder becomes necessary before a final logo is chosen, use a black icon with a white `PL` monogram and label it clearly as temporary in the asset notes.
